Lambda Coaching System

The secret to great results lies in trying to have zero unproductive sessions.

The system I've designed allows me to analyse your training data across every:

  • Every session
  • Every weight
  • Every rep-range
  • Every variation

With this data, I plan each session based on what you've actually done.

My Transformation

Why I coach.

I started weak. At 16 I couldn't do a single pull-up. At 23 I did 20 with 30kg added. I've done 10 freestanding handstand push-ups.

I wasn't a prodigy. I kept training. I coach because I want to help people like me get stronger faster. I'm still training. If I don't know something, I'll tell you.
